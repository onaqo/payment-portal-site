The model

You own the brand and the merchant. We run the infrastructure.

A white-label gateway is not a reseller agreement. Your merchants sign with you, log into your portal, see your pricing and call your support. AQ Pay is the engine underneath — maintained, monitored and upgraded without your team shipping gateway code.

01License & brandWe provision your tenant; you apply logo, colours, domains and email templates. Light and dark themes are supported out of the box.
02Connect providersAttach the acquirers, PSPs and alternative methods you hold agreements with. Our connector library handles the integrations.
03Onboard merchantsYour merchants apply through your branded forms; your team approves and configures routing, risk and fees.
04Operate & growMonitor, reconcile, bill and support from one console — then add sub-partners, regions or providers as you scale.
Multi-tenant infrastructure

Partners, sub-partners and merchants in one hierarchy.

Each tenant is isolated — its own branding, domains, users, provider connections, routing profiles and fee schedules — while you keep a portfolio view across everything. Resell to sub-partners who in turn manage their own merchants, with permissions scoped at every level.

Tenant isolation
Data, users, keys and configuration are scoped per tenant. A partner never sees another partner’s merchants.
Custom domains & email
Portal, hosted checkout and transactional email run on your domains with your sender identity.
Role-based access
Operator, risk, finance, support and merchant roles with granular permissions and audit trails.
Per-tenant configuration
Routing profiles, risk rules, fee schedules and payment methods configured at partner, group or merchant level.
Sub-partner resale
Give ISOs or regional partners their own branded console with a scoped portfolio.
Portfolio analytics
Approval, decline, dispute and cost metrics across all tenants, with drill-down to any merchant.
Merchant onboarding & management

From application to first transaction, inside your portal.

Configurable application forms collect the business, ownership and banking data your acquirers require. Your team reviews, requests documents and approves; the merchant receives branded credentials and a ready-configured account.

1
Application
Branded forms collect business, ownership, website and processing-history data.
2
Document capture
Request and store identity, registration and bank documents against the application.
3
Review & underwriting
Internal checklist, notes and decision; export packs for acquirer underwriting.
4
Configuration
Assign provider accounts, routing profile, risk rules and fee schedule.
5
Go live
Merchant receives credentials, API keys and hosted-checkout setup inside your portal.

Platform subscription & billing

Price your merchants your way.

Define fee schedules per merchant or merchant group — percentage, fixed, tiered, by method or by region — and let the platform calculate, invoice and report. Your AQ Pay licence is a separate commercial agreement; what you charge merchants is entirely yours.

Licensing is priced on scope — tenants, connectors, volume tiers and support level. We'll scope it with you in a demo rather than publish a one-size table.

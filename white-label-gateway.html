The model

You own the brand and the merchant. We run the infrastructure.

A white-label gateway is not a reseller agreement. Your merchants sign with you, log into your portal, see your pricing and call your support. TPP is the engine underneath — maintained, monitored and upgraded without your team shipping gateway code.

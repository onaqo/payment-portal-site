Privacy Policy

How AQ Pay collects, uses, shares and protects personal data on aqpay.services, and the rights you have over your information.

Last updated 12 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AQ Pay ("AQ Pay", "we", "us") handles personal data when you visit aqpay.services, contact us, or request a demo. It does not cover data processed inside the AQ Pay platform on behalf of our customers — that processing is governed by the agreement between AQ Pay and each customer.

1. Who is responsible for your data

AQ Pay is the controller of the personal data described in this policy. You can reach us about any privacy matter at hello@aqpay.services.

2. What we collect

Data When we collect it Examples
Contact details When you submit a demo, contact or partner form, or email us Name, work email, company, website
Business context When you choose to tell us about your business Business type, areas of interest, expected volume, markets, your message
Technical data When you browse the site IP address, browser type, device type, pages requested (in server logs kept by our hosting provider)
Usage data When you browse the site The pages you open, the website that linked to them, device and browser type, language, time on page, and the country and city your network appears to be in. Counted on our own systems without cookies. If you choose "Accept all" in the cookie banner, Google Analytics also receives the pages you view and actions you take; see the Cookie Policy
Preferences When you answer the cookie banner Your cookie choice, stored in your browser

We do not ask for, and you should not send us, payment card numbers, bank account details or government ID through this website.

3. How we use it and why

  • To respond to you — replying to enquiries, arranging and running demos, and sending the confirmation email after you submit a form. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering requests you make, and steps taken at your request before entering a contract.
  • To run and protect the site — security, debugging and preventing abuse. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a secure, working website.
  • To understand how the site is used — counting visits and seeing which pages are read, so we can improve them. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a useful website; the identifier that recognises a return visit, and Google Analytics, are used only with your consent.
  • To keep in touch about our services — only where you have asked us to or the law allows it. You can opt out at any time.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

4. Who we share it with

We share personal data only with service providers that help us operate, under contracts that require them to protect it:

  • Website hosting and delivery — our hosting provider serves the site and keeps short-term server logs.
  • Email delivery — our email provider delivers form notifications to our team and confirmations to you.
  • Analytics, only with your consent — if you choose "Accept all" in the cookie banner, Google (Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics) receives the pages you view, actions you take on the site and standard technical information such as your IP address.
  • Fonts and scripts — your browser requests some fonts and scripts from third-party content delivery networks, which receive your IP address as part of the request.

We may also disclose data where the law requires it, or to protect our rights, users or the public.

5. International transfers

Our providers may process data in countries other than your own. Where the law requires it, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

6. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and demo-request data for as long as needed to handle your request and our relationship with you, and then for a limited period for record-keeping, unless a longer period is required by law. You can ask us to delete it sooner.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. To use any of these rights, email hello@aqpay.services. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Cookies

We use only what is described in our Cookie Policy.

9. Children

This website is intended for businesses and is not directed at children.

10. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when our practices change and revise the "last updated" date above. Material changes will be highlighted on the site.

11. Contact

Questions or requests: hello@aqpay.services.

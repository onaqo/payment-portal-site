This Acceptable Use Policy sets out what is and is not allowed when you use the AQ Pay website, sandbox and services. Customers and partners are also bound by the acceptable-use and restricted-business terms in their own agreements, which take priority where they differ.

## 1. Use it lawfully

You must comply with all laws, regulations and card-scheme rules that apply to you and your business, in every market where you operate.

## 2. Prohibited activities

You must not use AQ Pay's website, sandbox or services to:

- process, or attempt to process, payments for illegal goods or services, or for a business you have misrepresented to us or to an acquiring partner;
- commit or facilitate fraud, money laundering, terrorist financing or sanctions evasion;
- test stolen or unauthorised card details ("card testing"), or submit transactions without the cardholder's authorisation;
- disguise the true nature of a transaction, merchant or business model, including transaction laundering or misuse of merchant category codes;
- infringe intellectual property or privacy rights, or sell counterfeit goods;
- distribute malware, or probe, scan or attack our systems or anyone else's;
- interfere with the availability or integrity of the Site or services, including by excessive automated requests;
- circumvent security, authentication, rate limits or risk controls;
- send spam or misleading information through our forms or systems.

## 3. Restricted businesses

Some industries can only be supported with specific licences, in specific jurisdictions, and with the approval of an acquiring partner. Others cannot be supported at all. Eligibility is assessed case by case — see [high-risk processing](/high-risk-processing) for how this works.

## 4. Sandbox and API use

Sandbox credentials are for testing only. Do not send real cardholder data to the sandbox. Keep API keys and webhook secrets confidential, and rotate them if you suspect exposure.

## 5. Security research

If you believe you have found a security vulnerability, please report it privately to [hello@aqpay.services](mailto:hello@aqpay.services) and give us reasonable time to fix it before disclosing it. Do not access, modify or delete data that is not yours.

## 6. What happens if this policy is breached

We may investigate suspected breaches and, where appropriate, suspend access, end a relationship in line with the relevant agreement, and report activity to acquiring partners, card schemes or the authorities.

## 7. Reporting abuse

To report misuse of AQ Pay's services, email [hello@aqpay.services](mailto:hello@aqpay.services) with as much detail as you can.

## 8. Changes

We may update this policy as rules and risks change. The "last updated" date shows the latest version.

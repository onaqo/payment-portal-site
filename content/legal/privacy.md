This Privacy Policy explains how AQ Pay ("AQ Pay", "we", "us") handles personal data when you visit **aqpay.services**, contact us, or request a demo. It does not cover data processed inside the AQ Pay platform on behalf of our customers — that processing is governed by the agreement between AQ Pay and each customer.

## 1. Who is responsible for your data

AQ Pay is the controller of the personal data described in this policy. You can reach us about any privacy matter at [hello@aqpay.services](mailto:hello@aqpay.services).

## 2. What we collect

| Data | When we collect it | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Contact details | When you submit a demo, contact or partner form, or email us | Name, work email, company, website |
| Merchant account and white-label applications | When you submit an application form | Your business and registration details, licence, the account you need, processing volumes and history, and your contact details, including WhatsApp and Telegram if you give them |
| Chat messages | When you use the chat box on the site | What you write, the replies, and any contact details you choose to leave. An AI assistant answers first and says so; a member of our team may take over |
| Business context | When you choose to tell us about your business | Business type, areas of interest, expected volume, markets, your message |
| Technical data | When you browse the site | IP address, browser type, device type, pages requested (in server logs kept by our hosting provider) |
| Usage data | When you browse the site | The pages you open, the website that linked to them, device and browser type, language, time on page, and the country and city your network appears to be in. Counted on our own systems without cookies. If you choose "Accept all" in the cookie banner, Google Analytics also receives the pages you view and actions you take; see the [Cookie Policy](/cookies) |
| Preferences | When you answer the cookie banner | Your cookie choice, stored in your browser |

We do not ask for, and you should not send us, payment card numbers, bank account details or government ID through this website.

## 3. How we use it and why

- **To respond to you** — replying to enquiries and chat messages, arranging and running demos, recording your enquiry in our sales system so the right person can follow up, and sending the confirmation email after you submit a form. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering requests you make, and steps taken at your request before entering a contract.
- **To handle a merchant account or white-label application** — assessing the application, contacting you about it, and passing your application details to the acquiring banks and payment processors that could provide the account. Legal basis: steps taken at your request before entering a contract.
- **To run and protect the site** — security, debugging and preventing abuse. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a secure, working website.
- **To understand how the site is used** — counting visits and seeing which pages are read, so we can improve them. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a useful website; the identifier that recognises a return visit, and Google Analytics, are used only with your consent.
- **To keep in touch about our services** — only as described under "Marketing messages" below. Legal basis: your consent, or our legitimate interest where the law allows it.

We do not use personal data for automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

## 4. Who we share it with

We share personal data only with the service providers that help us run this website. Each one acts on our instructions under its data processing terms, which require it to protect the data and use it only to provide its service to us.

| Provider | What it does for us | What it receives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vercel | Hosts and delivers the site | Your IP address and the pages requested, in short-term server logs; the content of forms and chat messages as they pass through |
| Resend | Delivers email | The details you enter in a form, to notify our team and send your confirmation |
| Google (Tag Manager and Analytics) | Website analytics, **only if you choose "Accept all"** | The pages you view, actions you take on the site, and technical information such as your IP address |
| Google Fonts and the unpkg content delivery network | Deliver fonts and scripts the pages need | Your IP address and browser type, as part of the request |

If you apply for a merchant account, we share your application with acquiring banks and payment processors only to arrange the account you applied for.

Enquiries, applications, chat conversations and visit counts are stored in AQ Pay's own sales system, which we operate ourselves.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with anyone else for their own marketing. We may disclose data where the law requires it, or to protect our rights, users or the public. If AQ Pay is ever merged with or acquired by another business, personal data may pass to the new owner under the terms of this policy.

## 5. International transfers

Our providers may process data in countries other than your own. Where the law requires it, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

## 6. How long we keep it

We keep personal data only for as long as we need it for the purpose it was collected for:

- **Enquiries, demo requests, merchant account and white-label applications, and chat conversations** — while we are handling your request and for as long as we have an ongoing business relationship with you, then for a limited period for our records.
- **Visit counts** — as described in the [Cookie Policy](/cookies). The daily code that separates one visitor from another cannot be linked to you on a later day.
- **Server logs** — for the short period our hosting provider keeps them for security and troubleshooting.
- **Marketing preferences** — until you opt out, after which we keep only what is needed to make sure we do not contact you again.

We keep data for longer only where the law requires it, for example for tax or accounting records, or where we need it to establish or defend a legal claim. When data is no longer needed we delete it or make it anonymous.

## 7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the GDPR in the EU and the UK, you have the right to:

- **Access** — ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
- **Correction** — ask us to fix data that is wrong or incomplete.
- **Deletion** — ask us to delete your data (see "Deleting your data" below).
- **Restriction and objection** — ask us to stop or limit certain uses, including any use based on our legitimate interests and all direct marketing.
- **Portability** — ask for the data you gave us in a commonly used electronic format.
- **Withdraw consent** — where we rely on your consent, withdraw it at any time. This does not affect what was done before you withdrew it.

To use any of these rights, email [hello@aqpay.services](mailto:hello@aqpay.services). We may need to confirm your identity first. We aim to respond within one month. There is no charge unless a request is clearly unfounded or excessive. You also have the right to complain to the data protection authority where you live or work.

## 8. Deleting your data

You can ask us to delete your personal data at any time by emailing [hello@aqpay.services](mailto:hello@aqpay.services) from the address you used to contact us. We will delete your enquiry, chat and contact details from our sales system and email records, and tell you when it is done. We may keep a small amount of data where the law requires us to, or to keep a record that you asked not to be contacted.

To stop your browser being recognised on a return visit, clear this site's stored data in your browser settings; the cookie banner will then appear again.

## 9. Marketing messages

We send marketing emails about AQ Pay only if you have asked to receive them, or where the law allows us to contact a business about services similar to those it enquired about. Sending a form or a chat message does not, by itself, sign you up to a mailing list. You can opt out at any time by replying to any message or emailing [hello@aqpay.services](mailto:hello@aqpay.services), and we will stop.

## 10. How we protect your data

We use technical and organisational measures appropriate to the data we handle through this website:

- The site is served only over an encrypted connection (HTTPS), and forms and chat messages are sent over it.
- Access to enquiry and chat records is limited to the people who need it to answer you.
- The keys that connect the website to our email and sales systems are kept as protected settings with our hosting provider, not in the website's code.
- We do not ask for payment card numbers, bank details or government ID through this website, so that data is not stored here.

No system is completely secure. If you believe your data has been exposed, or you find a security weakness, please tell us at [hello@aqpay.services](mailto:hello@aqpay.services).

## 11. If there is a data breach

If we become aware of a breach affecting personal data we are responsible for, we will investigate it, contain it and record what happened. Where the law requires it, we will notify the relevant data protection authority, within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach where the GDPR applies, and we will tell the people affected without undue delay when the breach is likely to put them at high risk.

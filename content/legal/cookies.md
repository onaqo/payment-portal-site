This Cookie Policy explains how **aqpay.services** uses cookies and similar technologies such as browser storage.

## What we use today

We keep this deliberately minimal.

| Name | Type | Purpose | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `tpp-cookie-consent` | Browser local storage (essential) | Remembers the choice you made in the cookie banner so we don't ask again | Until you clear your browser storage |
| `aq-vid` | Browser local storage (analytics, **only if you choose "Accept all"**) | A random identifier, so that a later visit from the same browser is recognised as a return visit | Until you clear your browser storage or change your choice |
| `_ga`, `_ga_*` | Cookies set by Google Analytics (analytics, **only if you choose "Accept all"**) | Tell one visitor from another and one visit from the next in Google Analytics | Up to 2 years |

The Site does not set advertising cookies and does not use any advertising service.

## Analytics

We count visits to the Site ourselves, on our own systems, and no analytics company receives that data. Separately, and only if you choose "Accept all", we also use Google Analytics, described below.

**For every visitor, without cookies.** When a page is opened we record which page it was, the website that linked to it, the type of device and browser, the browser's language, how long the page was in view, and the country and city your network appears to be in. We set no cookie and store nothing in your browser for this. Your IP address is not kept: it is turned into a one-way code that changes every day and is used only to tell one visitor's pages from another's on that day, so we cannot recognise you on a later day. Anything after a `?` in a page address is discarded.

**Only if you choose "Accept all".** Your browser then keeps the random identifier `aq-vid` shown above, so that we can recognise a return visit from the same browser. If you choose "Essential only", this identifier is never created, and one created earlier is removed.

**Google Analytics, only if you choose "Accept all".** We use Google Tag Manager to load Google Analytics, both provided by Google. They then set the `_ga` cookies shown above, and Google receives the pages you view, actions such as clicking "Sign up", "Login" or "Book a demo" or sending a form, and standard technical information such as your IP address. If you choose "Essential only", neither loads. They also do not load for a browser that sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal.

**When you contact us.** If you send a form or leave your details in the chat, the pages you viewed during that visit are linked to your enquiry so that we can answer it properly; if you chose "Accept all", earlier visits from the same browser are linked as well. If you follow a link to the Site in an email we sent you, that visit is linked to you as the recipient of that email.

**Not being counted.** A browser that sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal is not counted at all.

## Third-party requests

To display the Site, your browser requests fonts and scripts from third-party content delivery networks. These providers receive standard technical information such as your IP address, but we do not use them to track you across sites.

## The portal

The customer portal linked from "Login" is a separate application. It uses its own essential cookies to keep you signed in and is covered by its own notices.

## How to control cookies

- **Change your choice:** clear this site's data in your browser settings and the banner will appear again on your next visit.
- **Browser controls:** every major browser lets you block or delete cookies and site storage. Blocking essential storage only means the banner will reappear on each visit.

How underwriting actually works

Acquirers decide. We make sure you're ready for the decision.

“High risk” is a judgement acquirers make about chargeback exposure, regulatory exposure and reputational exposure. Each acquirer has its own appetite, its own prohibited list and its own jurisdictions. Our role is to match your profile to partners whose appetite fits — and to present it in the form underwriters expect.

Chargeback exposure
Dispute history and ratio, refund policy, delivery timelines and how clearly you describe what you sell.
Regulatory exposure
Licences, age verification, jurisdictional restrictions and whether the product is permitted where it is sold.
Business substance
Corporate structure, beneficial ownership, financial statements and operating history.
Presentation
Website compliance, terms, contact details and descriptors — the things underwriters check first and reject fastest.

Chargeback management & risk mitigation

Staying approved is the real work.

High-risk accounts are monitored closely by acquirers and card schemes. Dispute ratios, refund patterns and descriptor clarity determine whether you keep processing. The platform gives you the controls; our team helps you use them.

Dispute alerts
Pre-chargeback alerts routed into the portal so you can refund before a dispute is filed, where the scheme offers it.
Evidence workflows
Structured representment with the transaction, customer and delivery data already attached.
Ratio monitoring
Dispute and refund ratios tracked per merchant account against scheme programme thresholds.
Pre-authorization screening
Velocity, geography, BIN and behavioural rules tuned to your category’s fraud patterns.
Descriptor strategy
Clear, recognisable billing descriptors that reduce “unrecognised transaction” disputes.
Multi-acquirer resilience
Spread exposure across more than one acquirer so a single termination doesn’t stop your business.

Transaction management

Every payment, traceable end to end.

A payment is more than an authorization. The portal records each attempt, route, 3DS result, capture, refund, settlement line and dispute against one reference — so support, finance and risk see the same story.

FAQ

Merchant services: common questions

What merchant services does AQ Pay offer?+

Payment gateway integration, help arranging merchant accounts with acquiring partners, subscription billing, multi-currency acceptance and tools to search, refund and dispute transactions.

Can you help me get a merchant account?+

Yes. We help you prepare the application and introduce you to acquiring partners suited to your business. The acquirer makes the approval decision.

Which payment methods can I accept?+

Cards plus the alternative payment methods available through connected providers. Coverage varies by country — for example wallets, bank transfers and local methods such as UPI in India.

Does AQ Pay hold my funds?+

No. By default, funds settle from your acquirer or PSP to you under your agreement with them. AQ Pay provides the technology, reconciliation and reporting.

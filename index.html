Payment orchestration

The Conductor decides where each transaction should go.

One unified API in front of every provider you connect. Rules you define — by currency, card type, region, amount, method or provider health — select the primary route, and cascading retries on an eligible alternative when a provider declines or degrades. Pick a scenario to see the logic.

Risk & transaction monitoring

Every transaction passes through a secure gate before it reaches a provider.

Configurable screening runs before authorization: velocity limits, geography and BIN rules, device and behavioural signals, list checks, and 3-D Secure where it's required or beneficial. Flagged transactions can be declined, challenged, or held for review — and every decision is logged for your dispute team.

Gateway & API connectivity

Integrate once. Connect to every provider you need.

Merchants and tenants integrate a single REST API, hosted checkout or SDK. Behind it, AQ Pay maintains the connector layer to acquirers, PSPs and alternative payment methods — so adding or swapping a provider is configuration, not a re-integration.

Unified REST API
Payments, refunds, customers, tokens and disputes as consistent resources.
Hosted checkout & components
Branded pages and embeddable fields that keep card data out of your scope.
Maintained connectors
Provider API changes are absorbed by AQ Pay, not by your team.
Signed webhooks
Reliable, retried event delivery for every state change.

Security & operational controls

Designed so sensitive data stays out of your scope.

The platform is architected around tokenization, environment separation and least-privilege access. The controls below describe how the platform is designed; formal certification and attestation details are published on the Security page as they are completed — we don't claim status we can't document.

Tokenization by designCard data is tokenized at the edge; merchant systems handle tokens only.
Tenant isolationData, configuration and keys are scoped per tenant and per merchant.
Role-based access & SSO-readyGranular permissions for operators, merchants and partners; audit on every change.
Encryption in transit and at restTLS for every connection; encrypted storage for sensitive fields.
Environment separationSandbox and live are separate systems with separate credentials.
Monitoring & provider healthConnector latency and error rates feed routing decisions and alerts.

One unified API in front of every provider you connect. Rules you define — by currency, card type, region, amount, method or provider health — select the primary route, and cascading retries on an eligible alternative when a provider declines or degrades. Pick a scenario to see the logic.

Risk & transaction monitoring

Every transaction passes through a secure gate before it reaches a provider.

Configurable screening runs before authorization: velocity limits, geography and BIN rules, device and behavioural signals, list checks, and 3-D Secure where it's required or beneficial. Flagged transactions can be declined, challenged, or held for review — and every decision is logged for your dispute team.

Gateway & API connectivity

Integrate once. Connect to every provider you need.

Merchants and tenants integrate a single REST API, hosted checkout or SDK. Behind it, AQ Pay maintains the connector layer to acquirers, PSPs and alternative payment methods — so adding or swapping a provider is configuration, not a re-integration.

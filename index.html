FAQ

Questions, answered.

01The platform

What is a white-label payment gateway?

It is payment gateway technology you run under your own brand. AQ Pay provides and maintains the platform — processor connections, routing, risk, ledger, settlement and portals — while you own the merchant relationships, pricing and brand. Your merchants see your name, logo and colours.

Who is AQ Pay for?

Partners that want to launch their own branded payment gateway: PSPs, ISOs, payment facilitators and software platforms. Each partner onboards and manages its own merchants, and can work with referral partners who introduce new merchants.

Which payment methods are supported?

UPI (Intent and QR) on a branded checkout, and cards through connected processors. Merchants can also share payment links with a return URL. Availability depends on the processors connected to each merchant account.

02Payments and security

How does routing between processors work?

Each merchant can have several processors. Rules choose a processor by currency, country, card brand, amount, payment method and UPI flow. If a processor soft-declines or is unavailable, the payment falls back to the next one, and the platform is designed so a customer is never charged twice.

How is a payment confirmed?

By the payment provider, through a signed webhook or a status check. The result is never taken from the customer's browser.

Does AQ Pay store card numbers?

No. Card numbers and CVVs are not stored, and a UPI PIN is never requested. Gateway credentials are encrypted, access is controlled by roles, permissions and MFA, and actions are recorded in an audit trail. We describe our controls rather than claim certifications.

E-commerce

Cross-border stores live or die on checkout conversion. The levers are local methods, local acquiring, sensible 3DS policy and clear declines customers can act on.

02 · Subscriptions

Subscription businesses

Recurring revenue is only as good as renewal success. Card lifecycle, retry timing and dunning determine involuntary churn — and issuers watch retry behaviour closely.

Smart retriesAccount updaterDunning
RetriesSchedules that respect decline codesInsufficient funds retries on payday patterns; hard declines are never retried.
CardsAccount updater & network tokensWhere schemes support it, expired or reissued cards keep working.
BillingPlans, trials, prorationBilling logic lives in the platform, not in your codebase.
RiskMerchant-initiated transaction flagsCorrect MIT/CIT framing keeps recurring payments exempt from repeated 3DS.

04 · Travel

Travel & hospitality

High tickets, delayed delivery and multi-party fulfilment make travel a specialist acquiring category. Resilience across more than one acquirer is not optional.

Multi-acquirerDelayed deliveryDispute workflows
AcquiringSpecialist partnersIntroductions to acquirers that underwrite delayed-delivery models, subject to their approval.
RoutingExposure spread by designRoute by region and ticket size across acquirers; cascade on technical declines.
OpsLong dispute windowsDispute tooling built for cases that arrive months after booking.
FXMulti-currency settlementSettle in the currency of your supplier payments where possible.

Gaming & regulated industries

EligibilityLicence and jurisdiction firstWe assess what you are licensed to sell, where, before any partner introduction.

FAQ

Payment solutions by industry: common questions

Which industries does AQ Pay support?+

E-commerce, subscription businesses, digital products, travel and hospitality, marketplaces and regulated sectors such as licensed gaming — subject to eligibility and acquiring-partner approval.

Can marketplaces and platforms use AQ Pay?+

Yes. Platforms can onboard sub-merchants by API or portal, route each seller differently and report per seller, all under their own brand.

Do regulated industries need specialist acquirers?+

Usually. Licensed gaming and similar categories need acquirers that serve them, and eligibility depends on your licence, jurisdiction and the partner's risk appetite.
